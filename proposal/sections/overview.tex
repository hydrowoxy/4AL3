\section{Overview}
This project focuses on training a machine learning model to accurately segment brain tumors from MRI scans using a 2D U-Net convolutional neural network. Brain tumor classification involves identifying tumor regions within an MRI image. 

Automated systems have the potential to support doctors by providing an initial segmentation that can be reviewed by the doctor, helping reduce the time for manual segmentation. Further, automated systems can in many cases act as a proof-reader, providing an additional safeguard towards any false-negatives. 

Brain tumor classification is a challenging machine learning problem since tumors can vary significantly in appearance between subjects. Further, tumor tissue may also be similar visually to surrounding healthy tissue, or may only take up a small region of the MRI image.
