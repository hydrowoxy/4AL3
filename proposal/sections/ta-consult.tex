\section{TA Consultation}

We consulted Yasmin Ahmadi regarding our proposed MRI segmentation
project using the BraTS 2021 dataset. We discussed the available
data, including the four MRI modalities (T1, T1CE, T2, and T2-FLAIR),
and our plan to extract 2D axial slices from the 3D volumes for
model training. We also discussed the target segmentation labels
and our plan to use a U-Net architecture.

Yasmin recommended establishing a simpler CNN-based segmentation
model as a baseline against which to evaluate U-Net. She suggested comparing models
of increasing complexity to determine whether a more advanced
model provides a meaningful improvement in segmentation performance, rather
than evaluating U-Net in isolation.
She also suggested considering binary segmentation depending on
the final definition of our target labels.

Based on this feedback, we plan to retain the four-class
segmentation task defined by the BraTS annotations while
introducing a simpler CNN-based segmentation model as our
baseline. We will then implement U-Net and evaluate both models using
the same patient-level training, validation, and testing splits.
We plan to use the Dice similarity coefficient to measure overlap
between predicted and ground-truth tumor regions. This will
allow us to directly compare U-Net against the simpler CNN
baseline and determine whether the additional architectural
complexity produces a meaningful improvement in segmentation
performance.
